# MHAW Companion App
The MHAW Companion app has gracefully concluded its journey on the App Store, and I'm thankful for all the support it received during its up time. This app was developed for Mental Health Awareness Week in 2019, a week-long event organized by the Mental Health Task Force of UNC Student Government. The event featured a variety of initiatives, including an art event that encouraged creativity and reflection while promoting mental health awareness within the campus community. The app served as a guide to help users engage more deeply with the events and artworks, enhancing their appreciation for both art and mental well-being.
